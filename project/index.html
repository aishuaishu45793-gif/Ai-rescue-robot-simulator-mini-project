AI RESCUE ROBOT

When buildings fall and water rises, every minute decides who is found.

A single command center for disaster response: plan robot searches, track survivors, run rescue missions, manage teams and shelters, raise alerts and keep people safe with first aid and preparedness guidance.

In February 2023 earthquakes struck Turkey and Syria and collapsed thousands of buildings. Rescuers worked day and night to reach people trapped in rubble. Robots that can enter unstable ruins and find a safe path reduce the danger to the people searching.
Sample incidents, robots and survivors are shown for demonstration. Alerts here do not contact emergency services. In a real emergency call 112 (India).
